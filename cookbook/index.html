Cookbook of the Month: December 2025

Preview on Google Books

Baking and the Meaning of Life: How to Spread Joy in 100 Recipes

by: Helen Goh

Abrams Books, 2025,  2025

336 pages

  • Subject: Cooking, Baking
  • Cooking Level: Intermediate to Advanced

In her solo cookbook debut, Helen Goh draws on her experience as a recipe developer for Yotam Ottenlenghi and her upbringing in Malaysia and Australia to deliver 100 of her favourite baking recipes. From stunning layer cakes to savoury pastries, Goh’s creations reflect her philosophy that baking can bring people together and add meaning and joy to our everyday lives.

Why we love it: The holiday season is upon us which means it’s time to get baking! And while sugar cookies and gingerbread are classics, we want to shake things up this year. Whether you’re looking to impress your friends and family or just flex your baking muscles a little, Goh offers a huge array of show-stopping recipes and inventive flavour combinations to choose from that will have your desserts standing out from the crowd.

Best for: Ambitious bakers and high achievers bored with their dessert repertoire.

Our Favourite Recipes:

  • Pistachio and Dried Sour Cherry Scrolls, pg. 27
  • Potato and Rosemary Focaccia, pg. 41
  • Ottolenghi Lemon and Labneh Cake, pg. 127
  • Champagne and Blackcurrant Celebration Cake, pg. 200
