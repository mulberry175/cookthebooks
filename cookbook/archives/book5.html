Cookbook of the Month: July 2025

Preview on Google Books

Sweet Enough

by: Alison Roman

Clarkson Potter/Ten Speed, 2023

304 pages

  • Subject: Cooking, Baking, Dessert
  • Cooking Level: Novice to Advanced

Alison Roman brings her signature casual style to her latest cookbook Sweet Enough. Packed with intentionally simple and flexible dessert recipes, Roman’s sublime creations are designed to be approachable for even the most novice bakers, requiring little more than a few mixing bowls to pull them off. From sticky cinnamon rolls to salted chocolate pudding, Sweet Enough promises to effortlessly elevate your dessert game.

Why we love it: When the heatwaves hit we want to keep dessert as unfussy as possible. Enter Sweet Enough. No one does effortless chic quite like Alison Roman and no matter how hot it gets in your kitchen, Sweet Enough promises to satisfy your sweet tooth with as little effort as possible.

Best for: Anyone who has claimed they are “not a baker”, but knows that dessert is the best part of the meal.

Our Favourite Recipes:

  • Perfect Tangy Chocolate Tart, pg. 29
  • Salted Lemon Cream Pie, pg. 60
  • Hot Buttered Rum Pudding, pg. 150
  • Toasted Oat and Honey Cobbler, pg. 271
