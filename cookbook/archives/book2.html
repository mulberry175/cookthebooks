Cookbook of the Month: October 2025

Preview on Google Books

More is More: Get Loose in the Kitchen

by: Molly Baz

Murdoch Books Pty Limited, 2023

304 pages

  • Subject: Cooking, General
  • Cooking Level: Novice to Advanced

Molly Baz takes a maximalist approach to cooking in her latest cookbook More is More. With over 100 flavour-packed recipes, More is More encourages readers to take more risks in the kitchen and to cook with confidence. Recipes include breakfasts, lunches, snacks, salads, dinners, and desserts, and are ideal for both weeknight cooking and special occasions.

Why we love it: Molly’s philosophy of finding joy in the kitchen shines through in every single recipe. Her exuberance is infectious and every bite she produces is both delicious and achievable even for the most novice cook.

Best for: Mortadella lovers and maximalists

Our Favourite Recipes:

  • Marinated Zucchini & Mozz, pg. 90
  • Shells, Peas, and Buttermilk, pg. 104
  • Last Meal Scallops, pg. 174
  • Maple Ricotta Munchkins, pg. 280
