Cookbook of the Month: September 2025

Preview on Google Books

Mi Cocina

by: Rick Martínez

Clarkson Potter/Ten Speed,   2022

304 pages

  • Subject: Cooking, Mexican
  • Cooking Level: Confident Beginner to Advanced

Travelling over 20,000 miles through 32 states and 156 cities, Rick Martinez shares recipes and stories from his culinary journey across Mexico, highlighting the diversity, richness of flavours, and complexity of the country’s many regions. From Northern Mexico’s classic carne asada to tamales packed with shrimp, chiles, and roasted tomatoes from Sinaloa on the west coast, Mi Cocina is, at its core, a love letter to Mexican cuisine.

Why we love it: September may be here, but we’re not quite ready to let go of summer just yet. With its bold, authentic flavours, Mi Cocina’s recipes bring the heat and keep the summer vibes going even when there’s a chill in the air.

Best for: Those who like a challenge and aren't afraid of a little spice.

Our Favourite Recipes:

  • Tamales Oaxaqueños, pg. 110
  • Cochinita Pibil, pg. 148
  • Torta de Lechón, pg. 229
  • Tacos Gobernador, pg. 262
