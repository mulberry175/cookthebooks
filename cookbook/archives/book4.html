Cookbook of the Month: August 2025

Preview on Google Books

Salt Fat Acid Heat

by: Samin Nosrat

Simon & Schuster,  2017

480 pages

  • Subject: Cooking, General, Reference
  • Cooking Level: Novice to Intermediate

Part reference manual and part cookbook, Salt Fat Acid Heat takes an ambitious yet accessible approach to cooking by breaking it down into four simple, essential elements that anyone can master. Drawing on decades of professional experience and applying a lighthearted approach, Samin Nosrat teaches the hows and whys of good cooking, offering readers an arsenal of 100 foundational recipes designed to build skill and and confidence in the kitchen.

Why we love it: With Samin’s new book, Good Things, hitting the shelves later this month, we figured it was the perfect time to get back to basics and revisit her debut cookbook. Though written for beginners, Salt Fat Acid Heat offers cooking lessons and recipes that cooks of every level can learn from – and if it's good enough for Alice Water’s, it’s definitely good enough for us!

Best for: Beginners, food science nerds, and anyone wanting to brush up on their kitchen know-how.

Our Favourite Recipes:

  • Tomato Confit, pg. 140
  • Cacio e Pepe, pg. 290
  • Buttermilk-Marinated Roast Chicken, pg. 340
  • Apple and Frangipane Tart, pg. 397
