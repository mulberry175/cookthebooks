Cookbook of the Month: November 2025

Preview on Google Books

Ottolenghi Comfort

by: Yotam Ottolenghi and Helen Goh

Appetite by Random House,  2024

320 pages

  • Subject: Cooking, Comfort food, Middle Eastern
  • Cooking Level: Novice to Intermediate

From bestselling chef and author Yotam Ottolenghi, Ottolenghi Comfort is a celebration of flavour-forward comfort dishes inspired from childhood memories and travels around the world. With a mixture of quick and easy favourites and time consuming labours of love, this collection of over 100 recipes is filled with dishes that feel both nostalgic and new.

Why we love it: Grey November days call for weekend afternoons in the kitchen and belly-warming comfort dishes. Ottolenghi Comfort delivers both, with the bold flavours and international influences that Yotam Ottolenghi is renowned for – proving once and for all that comfort does’t have to be boring.

Best for: People who want to keep things cozy in the kitchen, but are looking to spice up their comfort food favourites. A fondness for tracking down hard to find ingredients is also a must.

Our Favourite Recipes:

  • Dutch Baby with Oven Roasted Tomatoes, pg. 22
  • Braised Lamb with Butterbeans and Yogurt, pg. 179
  • Caramelized Onion Orecchiette with Hazelnuts & Sage, pg. 202
  • One-Pot Chicken with Orzo and Porcini pg. 213
