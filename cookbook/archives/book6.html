Cookbook of the Month: June 2025

Preview on Google Books

Soups, Salads, Sandwiches

by: Matty Matheson

Ten Speed Press, October 22, 2024

368 pages

  • Subject: Cooking, General
  • Cooking Level: Novice to Intermediate

In this aptly named cookbook, Matty Matheson takes on three of the most overlooked foods from our daily lives and gives them a full flavour makeover. Despite their humble reputation, Matheson’s take on the classic soup, salad, sandwich trio is far from boring. With over 100 recipes to choose from, this book delivers maximum flavour for minimum effort.

Why we love it: As summer settles in, we don’t want to be stuck sweating over a hot stove for hours on end, but we don’t want to sacrifice taste either. Matty’s book promises we can still enjoy big bold flavours all season long even on those lazy summer evenings when cooking feels like too much effort.

Best for: Fans of The Bear and anyone who wants big bold flavours even when it comes to the simplest of dishes.

Our Favourite Recipes:

  • Irish Lamb Stew, pg. 66
  • Roasted Sunchokes, Fresh Ricotta, and Farro Salad, pg. 156
  • Porchetta, pg. 286
  • Not a Passion Flakie, pg. 315
