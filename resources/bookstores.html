Canada

Good Egg (Toronto, ON)

Good Egg offers a selection of over 300 cookbooks, cooking reference books, and works of food writing. Also offers a selection of cookware, pantry items, and lifestyle items for sale.

  • Events: Author talks
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic and international shipping

Appetite for Books (Westmount, QC)

Chef-owned Appetite for Books was founded in 2005 and features a selection of over 2000 books on food. They also offer private and group cooking classes, author events, and life sharpening services.

  • Events: Author talks and cooking classes
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Local delivery and domestic shipping only

The Cookbook Co. Cooks (Calgary, AB)

The Cookbook Co. Cooks is a one-stop shop for all things culinary in Calgary Alberta. They offer a curated selection of over a 100 cookbooks as well as kitchenware and specialty foods, and offer cooking classes and food and wine tours.

  • Events: Cooking classes, food and wine tours
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Local delivery and domestic shipping only

United States

Anchovy Book Co. (Saint Louis, MO)

This tiny bookshop in Missouri features a tightly curated selection of cookbooks, from timeless classics to fresh releases. They also offer a quarterly cookbook subscription box which includes a new release cookbook, a secondhand or vintage surprise, recipes and reviews, and little extras.

  • Events: Potluck-style cookbook club, private dining experiences
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic shipping only

Archestratus (Brooklyn, NY)

Archestratus is a bookstore and food shop in Brooklyn, NY offering over 11,000 books - from cookbooks to poetry books and everything in between - on the fact and fiction of food. It also offers groceries, Sicilian prepared foods and baked goods and a space for classes, book clubs, dinner parties, readings, and more.

  • Events: Book Clubs, potlucks, author talks and panels
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic shipping only

BEM Books (Brooklyn, NY)

Dedicated to food literature of the African Diaspora, BEM aims to be a culinary hub of global black foodways and storytelling, offering cookbooks as well as fiction, non-fiction, poetry, and plays.

  • Events: No
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic shipping only

Binding Agent (Philadelphia, PA)

Billed as Philadelphia’s only cookbook shop, Binding Agent specializes in contemporary and classic cookbooks as well as offering a selection of food-related memoirs, fiction and non-fiction titles.

  • Events: Book clubs, author talks, workshops, tastings
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic shipping only

Bold Fork Books (Washington, D.C.)

Owned by restaurant industry veterans, Clementine Thomas and Sam Vasfi, Bold Fork Books is Washington DC’s only culinary bookshop, and specializes in new and vintage cookbooks as well as offering elevated kitchenwares.

  • Events: Book signings and author talks, book and cookbook clubs
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Local pick-up, domestic and international shipping

Bonnie Slotnick Cookbooks (New York, NY)

Specializing in rare and vintage cookbooks and magazines, Bonnie Slotnick’s small East Village shop is packed with over 4000 out-of-print and antiquarian titles making it a must-visit for collectors and antique cookbook lovers.

  • Events: No
  • Online Shop: No
  • Shipping: N/A

Book Larder (Seattle, WA)

Located in Seattle, WA, Book Larder is a community cookbook shop with over 1500 new and imported cookbooks to choose from. They host a variety of events and ship internationally.

  • Events: Author events, cooking classes and demonstrations, and food-related discussions
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic and international shipping

Foodies Cookbook Shop (Stratford, CT)

A destination for food lovers, Foodies Cookbook Shop offers cooking classes and a small, well-curated collection of new and vintage cookbooks.

  • Events: Cooking classes
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic shipping only

Food Temptress Cookbook Store (Virginia Beach, VA)

Located in Virginia Beach, Food Temptress Cookbook Store is on a mission to amplify the works by underrepresented voices in the food publishing industry and offers a wide selection of cookbooks by Black authors

  • Events: Author events, cookbook swaps, and more
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic shipping only

Kitchen Arts & Letters (New York, NY)

New York’s long-standing bookstore dedicated to food and drink, Kitchen Arts & Letters offers a huge selection of more than 12,000 new and out-of-print titles. This much-loved institution is a must-visit for casual foodies and serious collectors, alike.

  • Events: Author events, cookbook club and classes
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic and international shipping

Kitchen Lingo Books (Long Beach, CA)

Located in Long Beach CA, Kitchen Lingo’s collection of books features new and vintage cookbooks as well as first-edition food writing collections. They also offer food workshops and classes.

  • Events: Food workshops and classes
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic shipping only

Omnivore Books on Food (San Francisco, CA)

This Bay area cookbook store located in an old butcher shop features a massive collection of new and vintage books dedicated to food and drink. In addition to their books, they offer a small selection of vintage menus and moulds as well as offering a quarterly signed cookbook club subscription.

  • Events: Author events
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic and international shipping

Read It and Eat Bookshop (Buffalo, NY)

This culinary-themed bookshop not only features a large selection of cookbooks, but also offers a wide variety of food-themed YA fiction, romance, mystery, and children’s titles to choose from.

  • Events: Book club, cookbook swap
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic shipping only

Vivienne Culinary Books (Portland, OR)

Portland’s cookbook store, Vivienne Culinary Books, proudly displays a carefully selected collection of new and used books as well as rare and antiquarian titles, indie food magazines, and kitchenware and linens in their unique shop.

  • Events: Author events, and cookbook workshops
  • Online Shop: Yes
  • Shipping: Domestic shipping only
