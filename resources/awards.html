Cookbook Awards

IACP Cookbook and Food Media Awards (International)

Every year the International Association of Culinary Professionals honours cookbook authors and publishers from around the world in 14 categories including Best American and International, Best Baking, and Best Book Design. They also present awards in Food Media, Food Photography and Styling, and Food Writing.

James Beard Awards (United States)

Among the nation's most prestigious honours, the James Beard Book Awards celebrate the country's top food authors across 16 catgories including Best Emerging Voice, Best Baking and Desserts, and Best Bread.

Taste Canada Culinary and Cookbook Awards (Canada)

The Taste Canada Culinary and Cookbook Awards recognize the best in Canadian food publishing in 5 categories from Best Regional/Cultural Cookbooks to Best Single-Subject Cookbooks.
