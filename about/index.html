About Us

What is Cook the Books?

Cook the Books is a brand-new cookbook discovery service for curious and creative cooks. Our goal is simple: help you find the next must-have title for your cookbook shelf.

Tired of hopping between multiple websites to find new titles to explore, we decided to bring all that information together in one place. And that’s how Cook the Books was born! We believe that discovering new cookbooks should be fun, so pull up a kitchen stool and let’s dive in!

How Do I Use Cook the Books?

Using Cook the Books is simple! We offer 4 main sections to explore, depending on what you’re looking for: New Releases, Cookbook of the Month, Book Lists, and Resources.

  • New Releases: Discover our favourite cookbook releases each month, along with the upcoming titles we’re most excited about.
  • Cookbook of the Month: Each month we highlight one cookbook we can’t wait to cook from; these can be recent releases or sometimes old favourites. You’ll find a brief description of the book, why we love it, a list of our favourite recipes, and 4 “read-alike" cookbook recommendations.
  • Book Lists: Browse curated lists built around a specific theme – from Cookbooks for Lazy Bakers to Chef Memoirs for Fans of the Bear. Each list includes brief summaries of the featured books so you can easily find what interests you.
  • Resources: Explore even more cooking and cookbook content including links to independent cookbook stores, food and cooking related podcasts, recent award winners, and a selection of fun playlists to listen to while you cook.

If you’re interested in learning more about any of the books featured on the site, simply hover over its cover and click to preview it on Google Books.

What's Next for Cook the Books?

Cook the Books is still growing, and we have big plans to make it even bigger and better for our users. Here’s a sneak peek at what’s coming:

  • Searchable Cookbook Database
    Easily browse and filter titles by cuisine, author, difficulty, and more.
  • Recipe Finder
    Search for recipes across featured cookbooks based on ingredients, cravings, or skill level.
  • Interactive Recommendation Tool
    Get personalized cookbook suggestions tailored to your cooking style and interests.
  • Regularly updated content
    More book lists, more features, more favourites!
