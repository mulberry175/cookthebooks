5 Cookbooks For Easy Entertaining

For those who love the idea of entertaining but hate the planning, these six cookbooks take the stress out of hosting with ready-to-go menus and plenty of helpful tips and tricks. So dust off your apron and gather your loved ones because your dinner parties are about to get a whole lot more fun!

Preview on Google Books

The Dinner Party Project: A No-Stress Guide to Food with Friends

by Natasha Feldman

The Dinner Party Project is all about helping you plan the perfect stress-free dinner party. Natasha Feldman not only offers over 80 delicious recipes to choose from, she also takes the guesswork out what to serve by providing pre-made menus, a fun flow chart of what to cook for when you’re feeling lazy, and all the tips and tricks you need to pull off a fun food-filled evening with friends.

Must-try menu: Old Jewish Lady Night

Preview on Google Books

Together: Memorable Meals Made Easy

by Jamie Oliver

Organized into 18 different menus, Jamie Oliver’s Together aims to make gathering with loved ones over a joyful meal as simple as possible. Whether you’re planning a weeknight meal for two or a holiday feast for a crowd, Together arms with you everything you need – from easy, make-ahead recipes to helpful tips, tricks and hacks to keep you organized – to pull them off without a hitch.

Must-try menu: Taco Party

Preview on Google Books

Sunday Suppers: Recipes + Gatherings

by Karen Mordechai

Originating from her dinner series of the same name, Karen Mordechai’s Sunday Suppers is a celebration of cooking and eating communally. Don’t be fooled by the name, Sunday Suppers includes seasonally-driven menus that range from breakfast in bed and packed lunches to holiday feasts and potluck dinners.

Must-try menu: An Early Breakfast

Preview on Google Books

Company: The Radically Casual Art of Cooking for Others

by Amy Thielen

Amy Thielen wants you to have more dinner parties, the more fun and relaxed, the better! Company helps you do just that, with a year’s worth of inventive recipes, twenty ready-to-go menus to choose from, and plenty of tips and tricks to help ease your entertaining anxiety.

Must-try menu: A Creative More-Time-Than-Money sort of Menu

Preview on Google Books

Now & Again: Go-To Recipes, Inspired Menus + Endless Ideas for Reinventing Leftovers

by Julia Turshen

Julia Turshen’s Now & Again aims to help cooks of all skill levels to bring friends and family together around the table. From intimate dinners to holiday feasts, it offers over 125 recipes and 20 creative menu ideas to work from. With helpful timelines, tips on how to be thrifty with food, and helpful recipes to use up leftovers, Now & Again makes cooking for a crowd approachable, enjoyable, and stress-free.

Must-try menu: Grilled Vietnamese Breakfast
